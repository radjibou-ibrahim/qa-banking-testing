# Test Evidence

## Objective

This folder contains screenshots and other supporting evidence collected
during manual testing activities performed on the XYZ Bank application.

The evidence was captured during:

- Functional Testing
- Test Execution
- Exploratory Testing

## Purpose

The objective of the evidence is to:

- Demonstrate that test execution was performed
- Support test results
- Provide visual confirmation of application behavior
- Document important validation messages
- Record transaction results and account updates

## Evidence Categories

### Login

Evidence related to authentication and logout.

### Account Management

Evidence related to account information and account switching.

### Deposit

Evidence related to successful and unsuccessful deposit operations.

### Withdrawal

Evidence related to successful and unsuccessful withdrawal operations.

### Transactions

Evidence related to transaction history, filtering, and transaction types.

### Manager Functions

Evidence related to customer management and account creation.

### Exploratory Testing

Evidence collected during exploratory testing sessions.

## Notes

Screenshots included in this folder are intended for educational and
portfolio purposes only.
